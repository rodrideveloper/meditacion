## Tips for Play Console Upload

1. **Screenshots (phone):** Upload at least 4, ideally 8. Add text overlays explaining features.
2. **Feature Graphic (1024x500):** Use dark gradient + app name + tagline: "Free Meditation Timer — Find Your Calm".
3. **Video (optional):** A 30-60s demo of setting a timer and the alarm ringing.
4. **Translations:** If your app supports Spanish (which it does!), create a full Spanish listing with translated title, description, and screenshots.
